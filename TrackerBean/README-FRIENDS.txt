TrackerBean - quick start
=====================

TrackerBean tracks how long you play each game, shows your stats, keeps a game journal,
and can turn your clips into highlight montages. Everything stays on your own PC.

Install
-------
1. Download "TrackerBean Setup ... .exe" from the link you were sent.
2. Double-click it.
3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
   click "More info", then "Run anyway". (The app is small and unsigned, so Windows is cautious.)
4. Click through the installer and open TrackerBean.

First steps
-----------
- Games tab: click "Scan for games", add the ones you want tracked.
  A game that isn't found? Use "+ Add game folder".
- Settings: turn on "Start with Windows" so every session is tracked.
- Playing CS2? Open the "CS2 Matches" tab and click "Set up CS2", then restart CS2 once.
- Have clips from Medal? The "Montage" tab can make a highlight video from them.

Updates
-------
TrackerBean updates itself. When an update is ready, a button appears at the bottom of the sidebar.
Click it to restart on the new version.
